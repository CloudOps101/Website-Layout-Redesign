Giving FAQs

Can't find your answer? We're happy to help.

Ask a question
Who should I make my check payable to?

Please make checks payable to “Prince George's County Community Development Corporation” and mail them to 11720 Capstan Drive, Upper Marlboro, MD 20772.

Will I receive an acknowledgment of my gift?

Yes. Include your name, mailing address and email with your check, and we'll send an acknowledgment for your records.

Can I support a specific program?

Absolutely. Write the program name, for example “Health & Wellness” or “Muzik Xpose' Radio”, on the memo line of your check.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Please contact us at 202-413-6431 or tolsonbanner@princegeorges-countycdc.org for tax information about your gift.
