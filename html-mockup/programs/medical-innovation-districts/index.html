Medical Innovation Districts

Prince George's County: a tale of two futures.

PGCCDC champions a Medical Innovation District (MID), a community-centered alternative to a hyperscale data center that puts health care, careers and clean energy in our neighborhoods.

The choice

Two very different futures for the same land.

The county is weighing what to build. Here is how a hyperscale data center compares with a Medical Innovation District.

Option A

Hyperscale data center

  • 50jobs created

    Primarily technical roles with minimal direct community employment.

  • $16.9Mprojected annual tax revenue

  • 820 MWpower usage

    Places a massive strain on the local energy grid.

  • 21,000+residents opposed

    Significant community opposition expressed through signed petitions.

Option B · Recommended

Medical Innovation District

  • 2,430+direct jobs created

    Career-level jobs with mandated local hiring: 40% Landover, 75% Prince George's County.

  • $76Mprojected annual tax revenue

    4.5 times more than the alternative.

  • 75 MWpower usage

    A 91% reduction in energy consumption compared to the hyperscale plan.

  • All 14County AI Task Force recommendations

    A community-centered solution that aligns with every recommendation.

Key components of the MID

What a Medical Innovation District brings home.

  • Healthcare Hub

    A 200-bed hospital and specialty clinics serving 125,000+ underserved residents.

  • Free Workforce Training

    Trains 1,200 residents annually in tech and healthcare, with guaranteed interviews.

  • Last-Mile AI Clinics

    A network of 9 small community computing and training centers.

  • Waste-to-Energy Power

    An on-site facility converts 300 tons of daily trash into clean hydrogen fuel.

75% local hiring

of MID jobs reserved for Prince George's County residents.

A community-centered solution

Careers, care and clean power, for the people who live here.

More than 21,000 residents have signed petitions against the hyperscale data center. The MID answers that call with good jobs, a healthcare hub and a 91% smaller energy footprint.
