Why we exist

From Oxon Hill to Laurel, we crisscross the county to make it a better place to live, for and with our fellow residents.

PGCCDC operates every day to give residents a real voice in the decisions that shape their community, through engagement, empowerment and entrepreneurship, building healthier, stronger neighborhoods from the inside out.

More about PGCCDC

Medical Innovation Districts

Health care, careers and clean energy, close to home.

PGCCDC champions a Medical Innovation District for Prince George's County: a healthcare hub, free workforce training and career-level jobs for local residents, powered by clean, on-site energy.
  • 200-bedhealthcare hub serving 125,000+ underserved residents
  • 1,200residents trained free each year in tech and healthcare
  • 300 tonsof daily waste turned into clean hydrogen fuel
  • All 14County AI Task Force recommendations the MID aligns with
Explore the MID plan
