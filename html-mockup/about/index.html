About PGCCDC

A real voice for the residents of Prince George's County.

PGCCDC exists because Prince George's County residents deserve a real voice in decisions about their health, their neighborhoods, and their future. We connect residents to better health outcomes, healthy food access, and fitness opportunities, alongside economic and educational programs that open doors to real opportunity.

Our story

We are here to make a difference, block by block.

From Oxon Hill to Laurel to Capitol Heights to Bowie to Forestville to Largo to Seat Pleasant, along with the other 20 municipalities, we will crisscross the county to make it a better place to live for and with our fellow residents.

PGCCDC also acts as a vehicle through its “Voice of the County” digital radio station, Muzik Xpose' Radio, to highlight and showcase our culture, concerns and issues affecting not only Prince George's County but Black America as well.

Reach out to discuss being a partner, a client or customer, or someone who shares our mindset.

What guides us

Engagement, empowerment and entrepreneurship.

Three commitments that shape every program, partnership and conversation.
  1. 01

    Engagement

    Bringing county residents into the conversations that shape the programs and policies affecting their well-being.

  2. 02

    Empowerment

    Connecting families to health education, healthy food and fitness resources in their own neighborhoods.

  3. 03

    Entrepreneurship

    Economic and educational programs, like coaching first-time entrepreneurs, that open doors to real opportunity.

Leadership

The people behind PGCCDC.

TB

Michael Tolson Banner, MBA

Executive Director

Michael Tolson Banner has been a community-oriented and civic-minded resident of Prince George's County for over 20 years. He attended Langston University and graduated magna cum laude from Clark Atlanta University with a BA in Communications. He went on to earn an MBA from Howard University's Graduate School of Business and holds a Series 7 license as a stockbroker.

Banner currently serves as National Construction Contract Administrator with NTERSECTION, LLC, and has also worked as a Senior Contracts Specialist with several federal agencies. He is the creator of MuzikXpose.net, a digital Black streaming music and news network showcasing the lifestyles of Prince George's County and Black America. Banner is also a recent graduate of the Prince George's County Neighborhood Planning Academy.

TH

Tanya Bender Henderson, PhD, MBA

Deputy Director / Chief Operating Officer

Tanya Bender Henderson, PhD, MBA, has built a dual career spanning Marketing Communications and Public Health. She has served as Director of Public Relations for a historically Black academic health institution, Project Director for a national capacity-building organization, and program developer for chronic disease management initiatives across both nonprofit and government sectors.

Dr. Henderson holds a PhD in Organizational Communication and an MBA from Howard University, and a BS in Advertising and Public Relations from Middle Tennessee State University. She has also pursued advanced study and research in HIV Science and Treatment at the University of California, Los Angeles, and in Human Sexuality at the University of California, San Francisco.

Board of Directors

AM

Alma McPherson

Alma McPherson holds a Bachelor of Science from the University of the District of Columbia and a Master of Science from Howard University. She has built a distinguished career in early childhood education and health care, spending more than 20 years serving children with disabilities and the families of returning citizens. She has devoted much of her career to expanding access to STEM education for young learners. She has also worked in public and mental health settings, advocating for policy change at the state and federal levels.

JM

John “Petersburg” Matthews

John “Petersburg” Matthews studied sociology at the University of the District of Columbia and attended the Oklahoma Technical Institute. Guided by the Booker T. Washington philosophy of equipping young people with a marketable trade, he taught leather goods and shoe repair in DC public schools for more than 20 years. He has also been a community and political activist for over two decades. Today he leads the Petersburg Shoe Academy, which teaches entrepreneurial skills to young adults across the DMV.

LF

Linwood Ferguson, CPA

Linwood Ferguson holds a Bachelor's degree in Business from Howard University and a Master's degree in Business, with a concentration in economic development, from the University of Maryland. He is a Certified Public Accountant. He began his career as a financial analyst and consultant with several Big 8 accounting firms, then moved into higher education, teaching accounting at local universities.
